Who We Are

We are Havas Bangladesh

We are a collective of creative and strategic communication agencies with expertise in advertising, media planning & buying, digital marketing, content creation and designing brand experiences. We are an entity of Impress Group – one of the largest and most diversified conglomerates in the country, with operations in the Film, Media, Pharmaceuticals & even the Garments industry.

Each of our specialized unit functions as an independent full-service agency while operating as a separate business, but we are at our best when we collaborate together to become the agency best positioned to help our clients create meaningful connections with their consumers.

For over a decade we have been helping some of the country's most beloved brands to connect with their audience by creating, sharing and learning to be better together.
